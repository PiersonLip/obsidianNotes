## Confidence Bands
---
[decent source](https://www.graphpad.com/guides/prism/latest/curve-fitting/reg_graphing_confidence_and_predic.htm)
A region graphed typically in conjunction with scatter plots with [[regression|regressions]] 

The band, and its associated confidence level (denoted as a percent), is the probability of another point being added the graph *landing* in the band. 

Note that very often most of the points graphed will lie *outside* of the confidence band, this is because it it assumed that said points have noise. I.e. its more-so the percentage chance of a new point (that *doesn't* have noise) lands within the band. 

Whereas a [[prediction band]] is is a prediction of where the points will actually "land" on the graph
