# Darwin instability [[Binary evolution in a nutshell (zotero-item-102)]]
---
Triggers [[Common Envelope]] 

To occur Darwin instability requires...
- Spin angular momentum of one of the binaries *exceeds* $\frac{1}{3} J_{orb}$ 
- Said star is tidally locked
- Said star is expanding from evolution 

This then triggers an unstable orbit which ***will*** result in [[Common Envelope|CE]]. As the star expands its rotation will slow down, however, the system will continue to try and keep the stars tidally locked, which in turn will speed up said expanding star, reducing the systems total angular momentum in the process, which will then reduces the orbital separation, which means that the expanding star must now rotate faster, and thus CE is inevitable.
