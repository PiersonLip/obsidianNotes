# Donor star
The star in a [[binary system]] which is giving material to its [[accretor|accretor]] partner. This process happens through [[mass transfer]], generally [[RLO]] or [[wind accretion]].

The mass of this donor star is what determines whether the system is a [[HMXB]], [[LMXB]], [[UCXB]], etc.
