# BH
---
A black hole. A point in space where the force of gravity is so strong that not even light can escape 

## Singularity 
---
The mass "in" the BH is in the form of a singularity, i.e. a infinitely dense point. This was predicted by [[GR]], however, it most likely isn't truly infinite, instead GR/gravity needs to be reconciled with [[quantum mechanics]] [@Gupta2026]  ^b5340b
