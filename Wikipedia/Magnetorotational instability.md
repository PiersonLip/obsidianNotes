# Magnetorotational instability [@Magnetorotationalinstability2026]
---
The main "drag" mechanism driving the material of an [[accretion disk]] in a [[compact object]] to fall inwards onto the object, leading to x-ray emission. 

The process of the material orbiting a massive central object become unstable and turbulent.
