A gravtionally bound collection of stars (or other point masses). These collections follow *Newtonian physics* [^1] 

Stellar dynamics is typically very closely tied with statistical mechanics, as the quantity of stars typically requires some sort of statistical analysis. 

Many of the mathematical methods are actually taken from plasma physics! 

[^1]: There *may* be systems that experience the effects of GR close to galactic centers, however, none have been detected yet
