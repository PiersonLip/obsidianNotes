# Milkyway 
---
- Contains about $10^{11}$ stars, totaling around $\simeq 5 \times 10 ^{10} M_{\odot}$. 
- Most of these stars are on circular orbits in a disk whos radius is around $10^4$ pc 
	- This disk has a thinkness of roughly .5 kpc
	- the sun falls roughly 8kpc from the center
- There is also a large quantity of gas and dust which render regions in visible wavelengths opauqe over the scale of several kpc
	- Most of this gas is *non-ionized hydrogen*, denoted as **HI**. Together, these form what is called the interstellar medium ([[ISM]]). The ISMs total mass is only around 10% of the mass of the stars, so has very little impact on the actual dynamics of the galaxy
	- It does however play a key part in the *chemistry of the galaxy*
- In the center of our MW we have a [[SMBH]], with mass $\simeq 4 \times 10 ^6 M_{\odot}$. This BH is typically called Sagittarius A*, or Sgr A*, after the constellation the radio signal was discovered in
- The largest component of the MW, by far, is the **[[dark halo]]**, which had radius around 200 kpc and a total mass of around $10^{12} M_{\odot}$. This dark halo is made up of [[dark matter]], and because it only interacts with our MW through gravity, stellar dynamics are incredibly useful for probing the properties of this halo
- Typical speed of star on the disk is around $200kms^{-1}$
