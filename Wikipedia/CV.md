# Cataclysmic variable star [@Cataclysmicvariable2026]
---
Type of binary with a [[WD]] accretor and a massive [[donor]], the material accreting onto the WD experiences a [[CV|nova]], leading to a transient brightness increase. 

## Nova
---
Caused when the density and temperature of the accretng material reaches a critical threshold, causing runaway fusion, shedding the disk/shell.
