# PISN

pair-instability supernova
 Beleived to happen to stars of mass between 130-250, where their end of life SN are so energetic it leaves behind *no [[compact object]]*. This means that theoretically no BHs between 45-120 should be able to form directly from SN. This is called the **PISN [[black hole]] mass gap**
