# Electric Flux and Field Lines

[[Class Notes/Physics212/Physics212|← Physics212]]

---

### Field Lines

- Measure of electric field lines over an area

> [!equation] Electric Field
> <a id="eq:ElectricField"></a>
> Measure of the energy in an electric field at a point
>

$$
E = k \frac{q}{r^2}
$$

> [!equation] Density of Field Lines
> <a id="eq:fieldlineDensity"></a>
> used when plotting
>

$$
D = \frac{N}{4 \pi r^2}
$$

> [!equation] Number of Graphed Field Lines
> <a id="eq:numberOfLines"></a>
>

$$
N \equiv \frac{q}{\epsilon_0},   E = \frac{N}{A}
$$

### Flux

Number of field lines passing through a surface 

$$
\Phi \propto N
$$

For a point charge aligned at the center at a sphere it can just be defined as 

$$
\Phi \equiv EA 
$$

To calculate total over surface …

$$
\Phi \equiv \int_{s} \vec{E} \dot d\vec{A}
$$
