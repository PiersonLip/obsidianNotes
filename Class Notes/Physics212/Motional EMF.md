# Motional EMF

[[Class Notes/Physics212/Physics212|← Physics212]]

---

Solving when the magnetic field is changing on the object.

If a bar is moving through a magnetic field, the protron and electrons will be pushed to *oppisite* ends of the bar, creating an *electric* field.

This field allows for a circuit to then be attached, in which case current will flow through depending on the velocity of the bar. 

There is a lorentz force pushing the bar in the *oppisite* direction of travel.

> [!example] Example
>
> ![[physics212-motional-emf-1.png]]

> [!equation] [[Equations/E Field of a Bar Moving Through B Field|E Field of a Bar Moving Through B Field]]
> <a id="eq:barThroughMagField"></a>
>

$$
E = vB
$$

If this bar is attached to rails that connect to a circuit, it will drive current through said current.

> [!equation] [[Equations/Potential Difference of Bar in B Field|Potential Difference of Bar in B Field]]
> <a id="eq:potDiffOfBarMovingInBfield"></a>
>

$$
\upvarepsilon = vBL
$$

> [!equation] [[Equations/Motional EMF of a Generator|Motional EMF of a Generator]]
> <a id="eq:motionalEMFGenerator"></a>
>

$$
\upvarepsilon = \omega AB\cos(\omega t)
$$
