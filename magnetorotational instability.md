# Magnetorotational Instability
---
[magnetorotational instability](https://www.damtp.cam.ac.uk/user/hl278/MRI.html)

The main "drag" mechanism driving the material of an [[accretion disk]] in a [[compact object]] to fall inwards onto the object, leading to x-ray emission
