## Little Red Dots 
---
First discovered with [[JWST]], they are numerous, bright, red, and very compact "dots" that were not previous predicted. LRDs have properties akin to that of a galaxy with a [[AGN]]. 

They exhibit "v-shaped" spectra, one side showing bright UV and the other showing red optical light, reminiscent of that from [[dust reddening]] or a hot dense environment. They also have [[H Alpha]] emission, akin to that from the [[accretion disk]] of [[AGNs]] and [[BH|BHs]]. However, they lack x-ray emission.
![[Pasted image 20260525235651.png]]

A popular theory for what they are is [[black hole stars]], i.e. black holes deeply embedded in hot gas, mimicking that of a stellar atmosphere.
