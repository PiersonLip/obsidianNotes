# Stellar Winds
---
Material shed from stars as a part of their process of stellar evolution. Whole bunch of stuff here, ([[MB|Magnetic Breaking]], the reason planets need magnetic fields, etc etc)

Proportional to the mass of the star and metallicity. 

ex. higher mass stars lose more of their mass to stellar winds, and higher metallicity stars loose more of their mass to winds
